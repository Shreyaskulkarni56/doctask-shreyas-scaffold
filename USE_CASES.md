## 🏆 #1 ABSOLUTE BEST MARKET FIT: Vendor Contract & Amendment Register

### 🥇 Why This Is the #1 Commercial Opportunity
Out of all analyzed sectors, **Enterprise Vendor Contract & Amendment Management (Legal & Procurement)** represents the **#1 commercial opportunity**. It offers universal enterprise applicability across all industries, dedicated SaaS budget lines, and a 1-to-1 match with DocTask's stateful LangGraph architecture.

* **The Problem**: A vendor agreement begins with a 50-page Master Services Agreement (MSA). Over 3 years, it evolves through Amendment 1 (changing payment terms from Net 30 to Net 15), Amendment 2 (updating SLA penalty calculations), and monthly invoices. Procurement teams struggle to maintain a canonical view of governing terms.
* **The Solution**: Living Master Services Agreement (MSA) & Terms Register with exact character-level citation spans (`char_start`, `char_end`) and human-in-the-loop review gating before section updates ($v1 \rightarrow v2$).
* **Target Buyer Roles**: Head of Legal Operations, VP of Global Procurement, General Counsel.
* **Target Buyer Companies**: **Salesforce, Workday, ServiceNow, Snowflake, Datadog**.
* **Where They Listen**: CLOC (Corporate Legal Operations Consortium) Institute, Gartner Sourcing & Procurement Summit, ACC Annual Meeting.
* **Door-Opener Pitch**:
  > *"When your team receives Amendment #3 for a key SaaS vendor, how do you verify it doesn't silently contradict SLA penalty clauses set in Amendment #1 without spending 4 hours manually cross-referencing PDFs?"*
