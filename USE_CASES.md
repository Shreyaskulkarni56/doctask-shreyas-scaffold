# SuperDocs Task 3: Real-World Use Cases & Buyer Companies

This document outlines 10 real-world enterprise use cases for SuperDocs, including target buyer roles, target companies, and the business value delivered.

---

### 1. Vendor Contract Amendment Register (Legal & Procurement)
- **Problem**: Enterprise vendor contracts have dozens of amendments, change orders, and side letters over multi-year periods. Procurement teams struggle to track current net payment terms and service level agreements (SLAs).
- **Deliverable**: Living Master Services Agreement (MSA) & Terms Register.
- **Target Buyer**: VP of Procurement / General Counsel.
- **Target Companies**: Salesforce, Workday, ServiceNow, Snowflake.

---

### 2. Clinical Trial Protocol & IRB Compliance Audit (Pharma & Biotech)
- **Problem**: Clinical trials generate continuous protocol amendments, patient consent forms, and IRB safety filings. Non-compliance risks multi-million dollar FDA regulatory holds.
- **Deliverable**: Grounded Clinical Trial Protocol & IRB Compliance Audit Report.
- **Target Buyer**: VP of Regulatory Affairs / Head of Clinical Operations.
- **Target Companies**: Pfizer, Novartis, Roche, Gilead Sciences.

---

### 3. Commercial Real Estate Lease Abstraction & Rent Roll (Real Estate)
- **Problem**: Real estate investment trusts (REITs) manage hundreds of commercial leases with complex escalation clauses, tenant improvement allowances, and renewal options.
- **Deliverable**: Master Rent Roll & Lease Abstraction Register.
- **Target Buyer**: Chief Investment Officer / Head of Asset Management.
- **Target Companies**: Brookfield Asset Management, CBRE, Prologis, JLL.

---

### 4. Enterprise Loan File & Debt Covenant Monitoring (Banking & Financial Services)
- **Problem**: Commercial banks monitor syndicated loans with complex credit agreements, quarterly financial compliance certificates, and borrowing base certificates.
- **Deliverable**: Debt Covenant & Financial Compliance Brief.
- **Target Buyer**: Chief Credit Officer / Head of Commercial Underwriting.
- **Target Companies**: JPMorgan Chase, Bank of America, Wells Fargo, Citi.

---

### 5. Architectural Blueprints & Site Change Order Register (Construction)
- **Problem**: Mega-construction projects face frequent site change orders, RFI responses, and engineering specification revisions causing cost overruns.
- **Deliverable**: Master Project Specification & Change Order Log.
- **Target Buyer**: VP of Construction / Project Executive.
- **Target Companies**: Bechtel, Turner Construction, Skanska, AECOM.

---

### 6. Insurance Claim File & Coverage Verification (Insurance Underwriting)
- **Problem**: Complex commercial property & casualty claims involve adjuster reports, police logs, repair estimates, and policy endorsement schedules.
- **Deliverable**: Grounded Claim Resolution Brief & Coverage Analysis.
- **Target Buyer**: Head of Commercial Claims / VP of Underwriting.
- **Target Companies**: Chubb, Travelers, AIG, Liberty Mutual.

---

### 7. Software Architecture & API Spec Alignment (Engineering & Product)
- **Problem**: Fast-moving engineering teams have architectural decision records (ADRs), OpenAPI specs, and PRD updates scattered across Confluence and GitHub.
- **Deliverable**: Unified Architecture & Interface Specification Brief.
- **Target Buyer**: Chief Technology Officer / VP of Engineering.
- **Target Companies**: Stripe, Datadog, Twilio, Atlassian.

---

### 8. M&A Due Diligence Data Room Synthesis (Investment Banking / PE)
- **Problem**: Private equity deal teams review thousands of target company files (customer contracts, IP assignments, employment agreements) during tight 30-day exclusivity windows.
- **Deliverable**: M&A Due Diligence Register & Risk Matrix.
- **Target Buyer**: Private Equity Partner / M&A Managing Director.
- **Target Companies**: KKR, Blackstone, Carlyle Group, Thoma Bravo.

---

### 9. Defense & Aerospace Subcontractor Compliance (Defense & Contracting)
- **Problem**: Defense contractors must verify FAR/DFARS compliance and flow-down clauses across sub-tier supplier documentation.
- **Deliverable**: FAR/DFARS Flow-Down Compliance Register.
- **Target Buyer**: VP of Supply Chain Compliance / General Counsel.
- **Target Companies**: Lockheed Martin, Raytheon Technologies, Northrop Grumman.

---

### 10. Healthcare Payer Credentialing & Provider Network Register (Healthcare)
- **Problem**: Health insurance networks manage provider licensing, board certifications, and malpractice coverage records across thousands of physicians.
- **Deliverable**: Master Provider Credentialing & Verification Brief.
- **Target Buyer**: VP of Network Operations / Chief Medical Officer.
- **Target Companies**: UnitedHealth Group, Anthem (Elevance), Humana, CVS Health (Aetna).
