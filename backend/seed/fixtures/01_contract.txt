VENDOR SERVICES AGREEMENT

This agreement is entered into between Acme Distribution Pvt Ltd ("Client")
and Northwind Supplies LLP ("Vendor"), effective 1 January 2026.

1. Payment Terms
Client shall pay Vendor within 30 days of receipt of a valid invoice.

2. Pricing
Unit price for Product Line A is fixed at INR 450 per unit for the term of
this agreement.

3. Term
This agreement is valid for 12 months from the effective date, renewable by
mutual written consent.

4. Termination
Either party may terminate this agreement with 60 days' written notice.
